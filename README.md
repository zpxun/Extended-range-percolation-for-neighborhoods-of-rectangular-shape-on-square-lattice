# Data and Codes for "Extended-range-percolation-for-neighborhoods-of-rectangular-shape-on-square-lattice"
## Contents
This package of files contains the numerical simulation data and codes from our paper of Physical Review E, (2026). It mainly includes the following three major sections.
## Part 1: Data in the paper
There are 10 data files, named from "datafig5.xlsx" to "datafig14.xlsx", which correspond to Figures 5 to 14 in our paper, respectively.
## Part 2: Data in the supplementary material
There are 19 data files, named from "datafigS1.xlsx" to "datafigS14.xlsx", which correspond to Figures 1 to 19 in the supplementary material of our paper, respectively.
