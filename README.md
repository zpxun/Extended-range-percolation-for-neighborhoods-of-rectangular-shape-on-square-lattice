# Data and Codes for "Extended-range percolation for neighborhoods of rectangular shape on square lattice"
## Contents
This package of files contains the numerical simulation data and codes from our paper of Physical Review E, (2026). It mainly includes the following three major sections.
## Part 1: Data of figures in the paper
There are 10 data files, named from "datafig5.xlsx" to "datafig14.xlsx", which correspond to figures 5 to 14 in our paper, respectively. The first four figures (Figures 1-4) in our paper are schematic diagrams and contain no actual data.
## Part 2: Data of figures in the supplementary material
There are 19 data files, named from "datafigS1.xlsx" to "datafigS19.xlsx", which correspond to Figures 1 to 19 in the supplementary material of our paper, respectively.
## Part 3: Codes
A total of seven program code files are included, six of which have the .CPP suffix and one has the .C suffix. These programs are respectively used to simulate bond percolation and site percolation for Models I, II, and III, and site percolation for Model IIa in our paper; the intended use of each program can be identified by the reader directly from its file name.
